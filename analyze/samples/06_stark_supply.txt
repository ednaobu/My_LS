SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement is entered into by and between Acme Corp ("Buyer") and Stark Supplies Co. ("Supplier"), effective as of January 1, 2026.

1. Purchase Orders. Buyer may issue purchase orders to Supplier for hardware components. Supplier shall deliver within ten (10) business days.

2. Term. This Agreement shall expire on December 31, 2026.

3. Price. The aggregate price of all purchase orders shall not exceed $75,000. Buyer shall pay within thirty (30) days of receipt of invoice.

4. Limitation of Liability. Supplier's liability shall not exceed the amounts paid under the applicable purchase order.

5. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

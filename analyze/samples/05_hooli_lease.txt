OFFICE LEASE

This Office Lease is entered into by and between Hooli Properties ("Landlord") and Acme Corp ("Tenant"), effective as of April 1, 2023.

1. Premises. Landlord leases to Tenant the premises located at 100 Main Street, Suite 400.

2. Term. The lease term is five (5) years.

3. Rent. The total consideration for the lease term is $1,200,000, payable in equal monthly installments.

4. Governing Law. This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Massachusetts.
